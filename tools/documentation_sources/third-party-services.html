Buyer responsibility for third-party costs

The buyer is responsible for all costs associated with third-party services, including Agora and Firebase. These costs are not included in the AppChat CodeCanyon purchase price. This includes any subscriptions, usage charges, overages, paid support, account fees, hosting, domains, certificates and other charges applicable to the buyer's chosen services.

Create and manage your own accounts and credentials. A free tier or trial is governed by the provider and can have limits or change. The purchase does not include prepaid usage, account ownership, a permanent free allowance, or third-party service availability. Set budgets/alerts and review usage in the provider's billing console.
